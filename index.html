Power: gas turbines

Tight to 2030

GE Vernova's backlog plus reservations rose from 100 GW to 116 GW, but only 53 GW is firm. It's taking 2031 slots and builds about 20 GW a year. Siemens Energy has 69 GW firm plus 26 GW reserved, with lead times of three years or more.

Exposed: GEV +10% 5dSiemens EnergyMitsubishi Heavy

Risk: Reservations aren't firm orders, and the GW figures use different definitions, so adding them overstates demand. GEV reported Q3 Oct 28 and I have no results. Research sources disagree on the earlier pullback.

Grid: transformers and switchgear

Tight, sources conflict

Power transformer lead times run 128 weeks (Giga) to 160+ (BADGER). Eaton's Q2 data-centre revenue rose about 65%. Only about a third of roughly 12 GW of US data-centre capacity slated for 2026 was under construction.

Alphabet: more searches, less money

Alphabet reported an earnings miss. Search revenue was about $63B, up 12% but below estimates for a second straight quarter, while query growth was the strongest in five years.

Free cash flow was negative $9B, three times the estimated deficit. The stock fell about 10% after hours.

Cut through it: Usage is up and revenue is lagging. That's the AI-search worry in one chart.

Opportunity Signals

Creators need an easy way to object to Meta's AI scraping

Artists and small businesses are angry that Meta's commercial terms let it use content from sites linked to their accounts for AI. The change was pushed to Nov 20, and UK and EU users can object via a form buried in Instagram's privacy center.

Simple version: a free walkthrough or tool that guides creators through the objection form. Open question: this only helps UK and EU users, so the tool's audience is geographically limited.
