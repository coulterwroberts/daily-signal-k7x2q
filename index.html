AI: The Wild Stuff

In one author's skin-cancer test, a 2015 image model outscored GPT-6 and Fable 5.1

On 200 biopsy-proven lesions, a fine-tuned Inception v3 got 125 right (62%). GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.1 got 84 and 85 (42%).

The CNN trained for about twenty minutes on one H100 and is small enough to run in a phone browser.

Cut through it: A single author's test on a six-way choice, using lesions hard enough to get biopsied. Specialist beats generalist here; it isn't a clinical verdict.

Power: gas turbines

Tight; Q3 on Oct 28

GE Vernova's equipment backlog plus reservations rose from 100 GW to 116 GW, targeting 125 GW by year-end. Only 53 GW is firm; 63 GW is reservations. Total backlog is $176B, and it is taking 2031 slots. Output is about 20 GW a year, rising to 24 GW by 2028. Siemens Energy has 69 GW firm plus 26 GW reserved, with lead times of three years or more.

Exposed: GEV -11.5% 5dSiemens EnergyMitsubishi HeavyAnsaldo Energia

Risk: Reservations aren't firm orders. AI capex financing worries, new suppliers, and mismatched GW definitions all cut against it. GEV is down 11.5% over five days at $815, but the pullback sources conflict and I have no multiples.

Grid: transformers and switchgear

Tight; data conflicts

Power-transformer lead times are 128 weeks (Giga Energy) or 160+ (BADGER). Switchgear averages 44 weeks. Eaton Q2 sales were $8.5B, up 21%, with data-centre revenue up about 65%. Bloomberg, via Giga, says only a third of roughly 12 GW of 2026 US data-centre capacity was under construction, with equipment shortages cited.

Exposed: EatonHitachiSiemens EnergyGEV (Prolec GE)

Risk: Lead times are consultant estimates. New plants could ease supply by 2027-28, and data-centre delays would cut orders. No price or multiple data was retrieved.

It showed no known psychoactive compounds, psilocybin included, so it may contain something new.

Health & Longevity

A painted-on liquid stopped cavities in kids

In a Phase III trial of 830 children under 6 (JAMA Pediatrics), 38% silver diamine fluoride stopped decay in more than half of treated baby teeth when applied every six months.

No drilling, injections or sedation. It halts decay but doesn't rebuild lost tooth.

Evidence: strong

Serendipity

Language leans toward safety

University of Vermont researchers analysed billions of uses of 20,000+ words. They say meaning is better described by power, danger and structure than by the 1950s valence-arousal-dominance model.

The new framework explains over 90% of variation in word meaning, against about 72% for the old one.

Confirmed
ScienceDaily
