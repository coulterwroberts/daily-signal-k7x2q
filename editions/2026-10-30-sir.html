Paxton's office sues Harris County over voter ads

Texas's attorney general's office sued the Harris County Clerk, claiming social media ads aimed at Black, Hispanic and Asian voters amount to racial discrimination. The AG's office says Paxton recused himself because he is on the ballot in the Senate race against James Talarico. The clerk's office has removed the ad tags and pushed back publicly.

Why you care: It's a Texas election fight, in the biggest county, in the middle of historic early voting turnout.

Power: gas turbines

Sold out for years

AI data centers need huge amounts of electricity, and the machines that make it are booked up. GE Vernova is taking delivery reservations for 2031. Its backlog of equipment plus reservations grew from 100 GW to 116 GW, with a target of at least 125 GW by year-end. Siemens Energy quotes lead times of three years or more.

Exposed: GEV +7.3% 5dSiemens EnergyMitsubishi Heavy

Risk: Reservations aren't firm orders (only 53 GW of GEV's is firm). AI spending could slow, and rivals are adding capacity. GEV was $887.85 yesterday, up 7.3% in five days and about 55% over 52 weeks, so a lot of good news may already be in the price. Its Q3 report landed Oct 28 and isn't covered here.
